Use cases

A few ways to set up Hyprtile. Every workspace is its own, so mix them.

focus

A minimal focus phone

  • Start from the oled void preset: true black, only the windows glow.
  • Make workspace 1 a free page with just the clock and notes.
  • Put pomodoro next to it.
  • Keep a black page for when you want nothing at all.

Recipe: a calm page

music

A music setup

  • A workspace with cliamp big and cava beside it.
  • Pick a radio station from cliamp: Lofi, Synthwave, Chiptunes and more.
  • Turn on real visualizer so the spectrum and cava follow the music.
  • Try the cava styles: radial and term look great on a black page.

Recipe: make a music page

work

A productivity dashboard

  • calendar and meeting for the day ahead.
  • notes as a todo list and pomodoro for focus.
  • world clocks for the people you work with, calc for quick sums.
  • The dash window greets you with the date and a quote.

Recipe: a work page

night

A night setup

  • Turn on day and night by the sun: a light theme by day, Tokyo Night or Matte Black after sunset, by itself.
  • The tokyo wallpaper and live weather, so real rain falls over the street at night.
  • frame rate at 30 and animations off when the battery matters.
  • Lock screen notifications on names, so message text stays private.

Recipe: day and night themes · Recipe: a rainy night look

apps

Your apps as icons

  • An app shelf as a 4x1 row of your four most used apps, under the clock.
  • A 1x4 column at the side of a page, or a 2x2 grid next to the weather.
  • Labels on, off or auto, and the dock's icon style: color, theme or mono.

Recipe: a row of favourite apps

rice

A full rice

For the r/unixporn crowd.

  • A preset as a base, then your own border gradient in hyprtile.conf.
  • One of the new wallpapers, like blackhole or topo, or your own image, a terminal font, tight gaps and sharp corners.
  • btop, fetch, pipes and matrix on one workspace for the screenshot.

Recipe: your own border gradient

monitor

A system monitor

  • btop for cores, memory and network.
  • gauges, network, battery and storage on the same page.
  • Turn on frame stats to see the frame rate too.
