Runbook

When something is not right: what you see, why it happens, and the taps that fix it. Quickest of all: open the menu, customize, search settings, and type part of a name. Still stuck? Report a bug.

fix

Home opens the old launcher

Android has one home app, and an update or a reset can give it back to the old one.

  1. Open the menu. If set as home app is there, tap it and pick Hyprtile.
  2. Or in Android: settings, apps, default apps, home app, Hyprtile.
fix

Double tap will not lock

The lock gesture runs through the Hyprtile lock service. Android, or a battery saver, sometimes turns accessibility services off after an update.

  1. Open the menu, customize, home, fine tune, lock gesture ›.
  2. Tap agree and open settings and turn Hyprtile lock on again in Accessibility.
  3. Back home, double tap empty wallpaper.

Fingerprint and face unlock keep working.

fix

Notifications are empty

The notifications window, badges and the music controls need notification access.

  1. Open the menu, customize, privacy and permissions.
  2. Tap notification access and turn Hyprtile on in Android's page.

Nothing read this way leaves the phone.

fix

Weather is empty or wrong

Weather follows the phone's location, rounded to about 1 km. With location off it has nothing to go on.

  1. Open the menu, customize, clock and weather, weather places.
  2. Type a city instead of auto, or add several.

A typed place needs no location permission.

fix

The screen saver never starts

Android starts a screen saver only when the screen times out while charging, and only if its own "when to start" says so.

  1. Open the menu, customize, lock screen, screen saver.
  2. If when to start says not while charging, tap it and pick while charging in Android's page.
  3. Put the phone on the charger and let the screen time out.
fix

Pro is missing

Pro belongs to the Google account that bought it, not to the phone.

  1. Make sure the Play Store uses the same Google account.
  2. Open the menu, customize, restore purchase.

A window with a padlock card is a pro window. Tap it to see what pro adds.

fix

The subscription fails

Insider runs through the Google Play app. A copy not from Google Play, an old Play Store, or no account on the phone can not reach it.

  1. Open the Play Store once and check you are signed in.
  2. Update Hyprtile and the Play Store from Google Play.
  3. Try again: the menu, help, insider.

It is optional, and nothing free or in pro moves behind it. To cancel: tap the same row, or Play Store, your profile, payments and subscriptions.

fix

Health shows nothing

Steps, heart and sleep come from Health Connect, and only the types you allow.

  1. Tap the health window and allow what it asks for.
  2. Or open Health Connect, app permissions, Hyprtile, and turn on steps, heart rate and sleep.
fix

Home network out of reach

On newer Android, reaching devices on your own wifi (hass, ping, link) needs local network access.

  1. Allow it when Hyprtile asks.
  2. Missed it? Android settings, apps, Hyprtile, permissions, and allow nearby devices or local network.
fix

The battery drains fast

Motion, glows and many live windows redraw the screen often.

  1. Open the menu, customize, search settings, type frame rate and set it to 30.
  2. Pick a calmer look, like eink paper.
  3. Dark themes save the most on OLED screens.
fix

Something is gone

Most things are hidden, not deleted.

  1. A hidden app: the menu, customize, home, hidden apps, tap it.
  2. A window: hold an empty spot, add a window, and add it again.
  3. Just changed something? Most menus have undo.
fix

Start again from fresh

When too much has changed, a clean start is quicker.

  1. Open the menu, help, tour and gestures, reset to defaults.
  2. Confirm.
