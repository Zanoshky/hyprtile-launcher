checklist

Before you write

  • Search the open issues first. If yours is there, add a thumbs up or a comment.
  • Check the FAQ and troubleshooting page.
  • One problem per report makes it faster to fix.
what helps

What helps

app version
On most phones: Android settings, Apps, Hyprtile. The version is near the bottom of that page.
phone model
Android settings, About phone.
Android version
Android settings, About phone, Android version.
window or page
Which window, or rofi, edit mode, settings, the lock screen.
steps
What you did, one step per line, so we can do the same.
expected and actual
What you thought would happen, and what happened.
a picture
A screenshot or a short screen recording. Drag it into the form.
your conf
For look bugs, the part of hyprtile.conf you changed helps a lot.

Your desktop shows notifications, events and messages. Before you attach a screenshot, crop or cover anything private. Issues on GitHub are public.
